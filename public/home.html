How it works

  1. Parsoid HTML gives the skeleton — real sections, typed wikilinks, structured references. Chapters are parsed, not guessed.
  2. Wikidata gives the rest — what the subject is, where it sits, when it happened, who was involved. That is what selects the template.
  3. A template pack per archetype decides the apparatus. A battle gets an order of battle and a casualty scale; a country gets a chain of predecessor states; a person gets a contemporaries axis.

No language model is involved at any point. Nothing here is generated — the prose is Wikipedia's, reorganised and never rewritten, and every book carries its revision, licence and author history.
