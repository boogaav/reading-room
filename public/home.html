Opening the room…

Every volume here is a Wikipedia article, reorganised and never rewritten. Its own sections become chapters, its infobox becomes apparatus, its links become the next books on the shelf. No language model is involved at any point — the whole pipeline is a deterministic transform of Parsoid HTML and Wikidata claims.
